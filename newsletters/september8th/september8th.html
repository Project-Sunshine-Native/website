Why and how was UTDR Wii released before UTDR 3DS?

This has been a question asked a lot, and the reason that Undertale and Deltarune Wii released before Undertale and Deltarune 3DS is because there isn't anything fancy, it's just the game running on a Wii. With 3DS, you have dual screen, touch controls, console content, multiple translations, and more added features. The Wii is also PowerPC, and since the Wii U is also PowerPC, we can just use the Wii U port with the 3DS optimizations. This is how we got it done so quick.
